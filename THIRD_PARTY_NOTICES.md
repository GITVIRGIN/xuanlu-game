third-party font, library, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, payment SDK, or
remote service client.

and must not be copied into its package. System fonts selected by CSS are
provided by the player's operating system and are not redistributed here.

immutable package. If that scan or human review identifies a dependency,
license, attribution, or source-offer obligation, update this document and

gate remains inconclusive.
